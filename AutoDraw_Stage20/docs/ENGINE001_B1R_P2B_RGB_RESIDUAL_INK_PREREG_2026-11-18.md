## Question and immutable provenance

Hypothesis: a **source-exact residual detail/ink layer**, computed from the difference between original RGB and simplified, source-medoid region color, can preserve readable eyes, mouths, hair and garments without bringing back legacy V44 strokes or ungrounded colors. Single primary test `testImages/wV6VeZI9S1Y.png`, original PNG SHA256 `07cc4568cb5e90d9514bc6f6c2112fee623f87f8fd0cea02fa517b04108e502a`, RGB 200x320 (y=320,x=200) source; independent original-only `full_features` (source Sobel90/delta4/ROI) before any 150x240 reduction. Original 4 rectangular ROI markers are the review areas, not semantic masks or inferred landmarks. The method selects **actual original pixel RGB values**, not invented RGB or trained recolor, for residual layer. Transparent source-over and LANCZOS antialias may form mixtures in final preview; these preview RGB values are **not** claimed to be exact original swatches or game32.

Legacy G32/P4c1, M1 masks, V44-B410 original/nearest32 strokes, old paint paths, old B1 color maps, P2-V1 frozen *artifact masks/labels* are banned as input/seed/fallback/target. Only test-only source math functions of B1-R-P1 (`check_source`, `full_features`, `filter_original`, `logical_rgb`) and P2-V1 **code** for original-only fresh `slic_labels` and `source_region_medoids` may be called. No P2-V1 PNG/NPY/JSON artifact read, no historical comparison image input. An arbitrary image path cannot bypass SHA pin. All new masks are recalculated from the original file on every fresh run; only original PNG may be read. Production, game defaults, Stage6, Monitor, Bridge, master unchanged.

## Frozen method and independent variables

One fixed baseline region map `J_FINE` from original: original-only P1 `A_SOFT` bilateral d5/color18/space2 under source-derived original full protected mask, then P2 original-only SLIC n_segments900/compactness6.0, sigma0, convert2lab, enforce connectivity, labels starting1, and exact original member RGB closest to regional median CIELAB (tie first row-major). Full200x320 flat medoid color map, logical150x240 NEAREST (same original-only mathematics as frozen P2-V1, but *fresh-run labels*). This base remains a control, **never** considered visually accepted simply because many colors were reduced.

For each original full pixel, compute original `CIELAB`, region medoid `CIELAB` and `deltaE_ciede2000` residual; original `L*` minus medoid `L*` is source dark-line residual (positive when original is darker than region base). Independently compute `source_detail_near=ndimage.binary_dilation(original_delta4>=4,iterations=1)` in original full dimensions; no V44 or learned map. Pixels may be selected as residual only if near that original contrast evidence. `priority` is the original-only fixed four ROI union from P1. Two fixed ablations:

| Variant | Outside priority: (`DE00>=` OR original darker L* by `>=`) | Inside priority |
|---|---|---|
| `K_RESTRAINED` | DE00>=11.0 OR dark_L>=7.0 | DE00>=6.0 OR dark_L>=3.0 |
| `K_DETAIL` | DE00>=8.0 OR dark_L>=5.0 | DE00>=4.5 OR dark_L>=2.5 |

Boolean expression: `ink_mask = source_detail_near & ( DE00>=t_DE(region) OR (median_region_L - original_L)>=t_dark(region) )`; no source threshold adjusted after seeing output. This preserves both dark structure and source-chroma contrast (red/tan details) where there is original contrast. All masked overlay RGBA colors are **exact per-pixel original full RGB**, alpha255; all other alpha0. Source-over on original-only medoid full RGB map, then full RGB composite resize to logical150x240 LANCZOS as preview; also retain logical medoid-only map NEAREST and no-outline original. The residual mask is a **source-detail reconstruction study**, *not* accepted Egret contour paths and not the old V44; do not use native brush assertions.
