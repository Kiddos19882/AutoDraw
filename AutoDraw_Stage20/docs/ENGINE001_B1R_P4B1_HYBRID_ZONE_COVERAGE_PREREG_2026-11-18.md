## New external research, interpreted as candidate geometry not proof

- [Region-Based Approaches in Robotic Painting](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-0752/11/4/77) (2022): paint edge/corners with constrained brush first; then unconstrained interiors, avoid nonrecoverable boundary crossing. Our original source edge/face/hair gates remain; don't assume physical pressure control exists in Egret.
- [A Framework for Adaptive Width Control of Dense Contour-Parallel Toolpaths](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2020.102907) (2020): fixed-width contour offsets can underfill variable-width cavities; multiple widths/medial geometry are useful. Hardware pressure control is *not* a capability of fixed-radius Egret strokes.
- [Optimal Guidance Track Generation: review](https://doi.org/10.1002/rob.22286) (2024): direction-parallel zigzags versus contour spiral; choose angle and overlap, avoid unproven complete coverage for interrupted/nonconvex runs.
- [Hybrid Strategy Selection and Polygon Decomposition](https://www.mdpi.com/2226-4310/13/11/976) (2026): per-region choose direction-parallel or shape-following spiral, do not automatically connect rings through concavities.
- Prior project generic research algorithms \`run_engine001_full_adaptive_fill_v43p4.py\`, \`run_engine001_v43p4_b3_contour_source.py\` and \`run_engine001_v43p4_b3b_owner_skeleton_source.py\` contain candidate *math*, but read historical source G32/V44-related data. **Do not import their candidate source maps or run those legacy planners as active inputs.** Reuse present P3D original-only runner, \`native_batch\`, \`renderP4A2BrushWidths1to10Probe\` and game-renderer reference only; generic source geometry may be implemented in bounded new experimental runner.
