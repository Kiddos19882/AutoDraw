## Engineering inference and one next step

E1/E2 show that **source-pixel geometry is structurally sound but source-only 80 independent two-point broad strokes cannot yet provide an acceptable full model image**. This does not prove every 80-stroke algorithm must fail, and real game CHALK depends on points, not stroke count. Most remaining geometry on wV and Hello is contiguous character clothing/hair/face rather than isolated dots; the model preview is an important negative against optimizing only total pixels.
