## Принцип общей системы

The task is **coupled, not independent**: foreground/background selection, color-region partition, assignment among fixed32 swatches, regional smoothness and original contour confidence jointly inform one segmentation-color graph. The model can supply **semantic features/region proposals**, not arbitrarily hallucinated RGB or game colors. A trained image/text semantic color-context model is **NOT available/validated** at registration time: **do not label a hand-coded ΔE/graph optimizer "neural"**. Candidate may initially use truly pretrained S1 SAM2 masks as weak features; explicit label `PRETRAINED_SAM2_PROPOSALS_PLUS_DETERMINISTIC_COLOR_GRAPH`, with honest sensitivity to their only2 regions. A true learned contextual color scorer requires pinned checkpoint/licensing, raw logits and separate candidate ID.

**Contours as soft guide:** use authenticated V44-B native RGBA alpha coverage projected only through documented ROI mapping. Important: internal V44-B detail marks are *not* guaranteed material boundaries, so do NOT turn every contour pixel into forced segmentation barrier. For neighboring candidate regions, V44-B alpha contributes to an *edge agreement score*, coupled with original image RGB gradient. On disagreement between source gradient and outline ink, keep V44-B unchanged and set contour cue weight to zero; record disagreements. Confidence only, not recoloring original native RGBA. Hard barriers allowed only where original RGB boundary and contour evidence agree.
