## Question and proposed algorithm

Can selecting local widths and directional lines, in addition to unchanged P2 contour rings, safely cover otherwise missed native owner pixels? The user's approved behavior: **large brush in spacious parts of the SAME region and smaller brushes in narrow remainder**, same-owner overpaint costs zero. A one-width-per-region rule is prohibited.

1. Recompute frozen source G via original S2b B→G (`nearest32`, `strong_edges`, `protected_merge`), original PNG SHA256 `07cc4568cb5e90d9514bc6f6c2112fee623f87f8fd0cea02fa517b04108e502a`, pinned game32 palette blob `461b5f3725b9b79888ad36e807b3cf4c9e445e98` and original BoardPane JS blob `74ad4d8ad162bb04bf8f1ff94860ef673c02512b`. Reuse original `plan_p2` exactly and append P3 candidates **after** P2; compare with P2 in **same native run**. P2's 55 strokes/1097 vertices and original game color RGB remain unchanged.
2. Source-only candidate generation: original G owner31/29 (240×150 ROI at logical board x155..304), each owner's distance transform and uncovered source-only P2 footprint model. Sample reproducible residual seed positions from connected components, residual peaks and spaced raster points. Try nominal native widths **30,15,10,6,4,2**, not one globally assigned width, and undirected orientations **0,15,30,45,60,75,90,105,120,135,150,165 degrees** (every straight 360° heading is equivalent to one of these modulo180; inverse endpoint order is identical geometry; no false angular quantization claim). Use original owner-mask distance to limit source model centerlines. Require the full rasterized centerline of every candidate to lie within its original owner and source model footprint outside owner to remain within3 logical px. Every candidate is 2 points, geometrically bounded, no turn inside same stroke; 0.25px width2 micro segments may be included only as strictly bounded residual fallback and separately counted. Same-color overlap has zero *penalty* and may occur freely.
3. Greedy P3 addition chooses largest **new uncovered own-source pixels per synthetic vertex** across all widths, angles, and both owners; tie deterministic by gain then owner, width, direction, seed index. After each selection recompute gain on remaining model mask; do not select paths with zero new source gain. Score avoids favoring any one width in advance. Candidates are for **source E1/E2 planning only**, not equivalent to original Egret rasterization or measured coverage. Original P2 model footprint may over/underestimate actual Egret, and that discrepancy must be retained.
